Personal data is deleted within 14 days after an account deletion request.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to the ChodhiQ account you want to remove.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Privacy & your data.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion by email

Email chodhiq@gmail.com from the address associated with the account, or explain if you cannot access it. Do not include your password. ChodhiQ operates online from India and has no physical address; email is the contact method.

The app's deletion flow invokes the `delete-account` server function. Personal data is deleted within 14 days after an account deletion request. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

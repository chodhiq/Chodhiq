# ChodhiQ moderation and complaints policy

**Status:** Operational draft requiring Online Safety Act scope/risk assessment and legal approval.  
**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** `{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}`

## Scope and principles

This policy covers reviews, replies, community posts, provider listings, profiles, images, messages reported to ChodhiQ and account conduct. Decisions must be lawful, consistent, proportionate, privacy-conscious and recorded in an audit trail.

## Prohibited content and conduct

- Illegal content or activity, including credible threats, terrorism content, child sexual exploitation or abuse, fraud and unlawful sale of regulated goods.
- Harassment, hateful abuse, stalking, doxxing, non-consensual intimate material or exploitation.
- Deceptive provider identities, fabricated reviews, scams, impersonation or materially misleading advertising.
- Infringing content, malware, spam, unauthorised commercial solicitation or attempts to defeat safety controls.
- Personal or verification information published without authority.

## Reporting and triage

Users may use in-product reporting or contact `{{MODERATION_EMAIL}}`. Reports should receive an acknowledgement within `{{ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_TARGET}}`. Reviewers classify urgent safety/illegal-content reports immediately, serious fraud or privacy reports as priority, and ordinary rules disputes in the normal queue. Emergency threats should also be reported to emergency services.

## Actions

ChodhiQ may leave content unchanged; reduce visibility; add context; remove content; restrict a feature; suspend or terminate an account; preserve evidence; notify affected users; or refer matters to competent authorities. Reviewers use the least restrictive effective action and record the rule, evidence, decision and duration. Automated tools, if introduced, must be described in the public terms and subject to human review appropriate to risk.

## Notice and appeals

Except where prohibited or unsafe, the affected user receives the content/account reference, rule, action, duration and appeal route. Appeals may be submitted to `{{APPEALS_EMAIL}}` within `{{APPEAL_WINDOW}}`. A reviewer not responsible for the original decision should determine the appeal within `{{APPEAL_TARGET}}`. Reinstatement or correction is recorded and communicated.

## Evidence, privacy and transparency

Access is limited to authorised reviewers. Evidence is retained according to the retention schedule and longer only when required for safety, litigation or law enforcement. ChodhiQ will publish required transparency information and maintain the illegal-content and children's-access risk assessments applicable to its service.

**Short description (58/80 characters):** Discover South Asian businesses, connect with providers and book.

**Full description:**

Discover businesses and services with ChodhiQ. Explore providers by category and location, save businesses for later, and contact providers about services and availability.

Create an account to request bookings, manage your requests, and receive updates. Share reviews and take part in community spaces with tools for posts, comments and photos.

Providers can claim eligible listings and manage their business information through the provider web dashboard. Provider booking and business features may require separate eligibility or verification.

Availability and booking requests are subject to provider confirmation. Contact providers directly to confirm details before making plans.

**App access / reviewer instructions:**

1. Install the app from the Play testing link on an Android device.
2. On the welcome screen choose **Continue with Google** and select the reviewer Google account supplied to Play Console. Email confirmation is off for the beta.
3. To inspect customer discovery, browse categories, open a provider, save it, and view its available booking times. Do not submit a real-world booking without an approved test provider/account.
4. For authenticated customer features, use the test account below. Credentials must be added by the owner in Play Console; do not place a password in this repository.

## Information we use

- Account information: name, email address, authentication identifiers, profile details and preferences.
- Marketplace activity: saved providers, quote requests, bookings, reviews, reports, community posts and communications submitted through ChodhiQ.
- Provider information: business details, services, ownership claims, application records and private verification evidence.
- Device and location information: approximate location or selected city, push-notification token, device/platform information, consent state and limited diagnostic data.
- Transaction information: subscription or payment identifiers and status. Payment-card details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by ChodhiQ.
- Support, safety and audit information: support messages, moderation decisions, access records and security events.

We do not intentionally collect special-category information. Do not include sensitive personal information in free-text fields or uploads unless it is specifically required for an authorised provider-verification process.

## Sharing and processors

We share information only as needed with hosting/database, application delivery, error monitoring, notification, payment, advertising and customer-support providers; with providers and customers participating in a requested transaction; with professional advisers; and with authorities where legally required. The production vendor register and international-transfer safeguards must be approved before publication. We require processors to protect information and use it only for instructed purposes.

Public reviews, community posts, profile fields and provider listings may be visible to other users. Private quotes, bookings and verification documents are restricted to authorised participants and reviewers.

We follow the published [retention schedule](./RETENTION_POLICY.md). ## Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Use **Settings → Privacy & your data** to export data, manage advertising choices or delete your account, or contact `{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}`. We may verify your identity before acting.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/), but please contact us first so we can try to resolve the concern.

## Account deletion

In-app deletion removes the account and associated profile, saved items, bookings, reviews, claims, community content and verification uploads unless a specific record must be retained by law or for a documented legal claim. Any retained record is isolated from ordinary product use and deleted when its exception expires. Instructions are available at `{{PUBLIC_BASE_URL}}/delete-account`.

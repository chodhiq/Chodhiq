## Terms checklist

- Identify operator, eligibility, account security and acceptable use.
- Explain directory information, provider independence and absence of endorsement.
- State booking/quote responsibilities, cancellations and dispute routes.
- Describe paid provider products, renewal, cancellation, refunds and taxes.
- Cover user content rights, licence, reports, moderation and appeals.
- Set liability, availability, termination, governing law and complaint process.
- Record version, effective date and material-change notification process.

## Provider agreement checklist

- Provider warrants authority, accurate details, required licences and insurance.
- Provider controls prices, availability, fulfilment, refunds and consumer-law duties.
- Provider grants a limited licence for approved business content and images.
- ChodhiQ may verify, pause, correct or remove misleading/unsafe listings.
- Advertising claims must be substantiated; campaign dates and inventory are controlled server-side.
- Data received through leads/bookings may be used only to fulfil the requested service.

## Moderation rules checklist

- Publish clear prohibited-content categories and reporting routes.
- Use least-privilege reviewer access, required reasons and immutable audit records.
- Define emergency removal, normal review targets, notice, appeal and repeat-abuse handling.
- Preserve evidence only as long as required and restrict access to authorised reviewers.

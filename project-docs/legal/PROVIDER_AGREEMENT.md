**Status:** Publication candidate requiring legal approval.

This agreement supplements the ChodhiQ Terms and applies to each business or person listing services ("Provider").

## Provider promises

The Provider confirms that it has authority to enter this agreement and will:

- keep its identity, contact information, services, availability, prices and claims accurate;
- maintain all licences, registrations, permissions, qualifications and insurance required for its work;
- comply with consumer, equality, advertising, privacy, tax, employment and sector-specific law;
- give customers clear pre-contract information, cancellation/refund terms and receipts where required;
- use customer information received through ChodhiQ only to answer or fulfil the customer's request;
- protect account access and promptly report suspected compromise, unsafe conduct or material changes.

## Verification and listings

The Provider authorises ChodhiQ to review submitted evidence and contact relevant sources where lawful. Verification is limited, time-specific and may be refused, paused or withdrawn. ChodhiQ may correct, label, restrict or remove inaccurate, misleading, expired, unsafe or unlawful listings. The Provider grants ChodhiQ a non-exclusive licence to display approved business names, marks, descriptions and images for operating and promoting the marketplace.

## Leads, bookings and customer relationships

The Provider is solely responsible for quotes, pricing, acceptance, performance, staff/subcontractors, customer communications, cancellations, refunds and disputes. ChodhiQ does not guarantee ranking, enquiries, bookings, revenue or exclusivity. Providers must not sell, reuse or combine customer lead information for unrelated marketing without a separate lawful basis and required consent.

## Reviews, moderation and advertising

Providers must not offer incentives for misleading reviews, review their own business, retaliate against reviewers or suppress legitimate feedback. Replies must comply with the moderation policy. Advertising and promotional claims must be accurate, substantiated, identifiable and within approved campaign dates.

## Fees, suspension and termination

Paid products, if activated, will be governed by the displayed order terms. ChodhiQ may proportionately limit or suspend access during an investigation and may terminate for serious or repeated breach, fraud, safety risk, non-payment or legal necessity. The Provider may close its account subject to unresolved transactions and lawful retention.

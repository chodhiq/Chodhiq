## Play form questions to resolve

- Confirm phone visibility in provider workflows, Sentry retention and IP handling, and which data SDKs collect before user interaction or transmit despite disabled feature flags.
- Confirm whether each category is linked to user identity, encrypted in transit, required or optional, and retention/deletion behavior in deployed Production.
- Confirm processor/subprocessor and transfer disclosures for Supabase, Firebase/FCM, Google Sign-In, maps, Expo and Sentry (if enabled).
- Authentication email: the published beta notice states that Resend receives recipient email addresses, message content and delivery logs; storage is in the US, despite an Ireland sending region; Free-plan retention is 30 days while the account is active, with 7-day backups. Verify the live Resend plan/settings and disclose actual processing accurately. Google Play permits excluding transfers to qualifying service providers from the “sharing” declaration, but collection/use may still require declaration; verify against current Play instructions and the provider relationship.
- Confirm whether provider-facing booking/contact details count as sharing with third parties and describe the user's action that initiates it.
- Confirm whether account deletion removes all categories and what legally or operationally retained data remains.
- Payments and Google banner ads are disabled in `beta-play`; verify the final binary and Play declarations reflect this.

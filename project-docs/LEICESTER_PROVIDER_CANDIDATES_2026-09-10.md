## Publication gate

Before any candidate becomes a public provider listing, record:

1. Provider confirmation that the phone, email, address and services are current.
2. Explicit permission to publish the listing on ChodhiQ.
3. Written permission or a compatible licence for the exact image file.
4. The approved image's source URL, approval reference and approval date.
5. A final postcode check confirming the business is within the intended Leicester test area.

Until those checks are complete, keep these records in a private candidate/research
state rather than inserting them into `public.providers`.

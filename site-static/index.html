04HOW PEOPLE USE CHODHIQ
FOR CUSTOMERS & COMMUNITY⌕

Find local services.
Stay connected.

Use the ChodhiQ app to discover South Indian businesses and independent professionals across the UK.

  • Search by service, category and area, or explore nearby places on the map.
  • Check the information providers share: services, prices, hours, photos and customer reviews.
  • Save favourites, contact providers, send quote enquiries and request bookings where available.
  • Take part in community conversations, share recommendations and explore local events and offers.
See how discovery works → ✳✦
FOR BUSINESSES & PROFESSIONALS↗

Manage your listing.
Respond to customers.

ChodhiQ gives approved providers an app listing and a separate web dashboard for day-to-day business activity.

  • Claim an existing listing or create one, submit business details and proof of UK trading for administrator review.
  • After approval, update your profile, photos, contact details, services, starting prices and opening hours.
  • Receive customer enquiries and booking requests, reply to leads and reviews, and manage booking decisions.
  • See listing activity such as profile views, calls, website visits, directions, saves and shares.

Beta: provider access follows administrator approval. ChodhiQ payments and paid provider subscriptions are currently disabled.

What is ChodhiQ? +

ChodhiQ is a community-first discovery platform connecting the South Indian community in the UK with local businesses, independent professionals and one another.

Who can use ChodhiQ? +

The current beta is intended for people in the United Kingdom. ChodhiQ is designed around the South Indian community and welcomes people who want to discover, participate and connect respectfully.

How do I find a business or professional? +

Explore providers by category and area in the ChodhiQ app. You can view profile information and contact providers directly. Providers are independent, so confirm their availability, service details and terms with them.

Does ChodhiQ guarantee a provider or listing? +

No. Providers are independent. A listing or verification label is not a guarantee of service quality, qualifications, insurance or future conduct. Please check suitability before making arrangements.

Is ChodhiQ free? +

Customers can use the beta’s discovery and community features without a ChodhiQ subscription. ChodhiQ payment collection and provider subscription checkout are currently disabled.

How does ChodhiQ handle my information? +

We explain the information the beta uses, why we use it, and the choices available to you in our Privacy Notice.
